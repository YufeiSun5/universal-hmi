is decorative, not an astronomical observation. No NASA logo is used.

Sentinel-2 Level-2A true-color imagery - Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2025).
https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice
COG conversion and hosting: Element 84 / Earth Search, AWS Open Data.
https://registry.opendata.aws/sentinel-2-l2a-cogs/
Scene IDs, acquisition timestamps, source URLs, SHA-256 and processing description:
imagery/manifest.json. Raster changes: reprojection, clipping, resampling, feathered
WebP encoding (JPEG overviews). Zhuanghe retains native 10 m detail; Dalian is
downsampled to approximately 120 m. Footprints are rectangles, not legal boundaries.
No new observational information or enhanced native resolution is claimed.

Sentinel-2 same-date SCL class 6 is additionally used for the decorative sea mask.
Two pinned SCL COG source URLs and hashes are in imagery/water-source.json.
Changes: nearest geographic reprojection, south-edge-connected water selection,
conservative shoreline erosion/feather. It is not a surveyed coastline.
sea-normal.png is procedurally generated by this project. Animated shading is
decorative, not live sea state, wind, tide or actual solar illumination.
